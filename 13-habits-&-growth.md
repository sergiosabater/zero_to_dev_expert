## 🧠 Part 1 · Productivity Without Burnout

### ⏱️ The Pomodoro Technique

**Classic Pomodoro:**

* 🍅 25 minutes focused work
* ⏸️ 5 minutes break
* 🔁 Repeat 4 times
* 🛑 Long break (15–30 min)

✅ Why it works:

* Prevents mental fatigue
* Reduces procrastination
* Makes big tasks feel manageable

❌ Bad:

> “I’ll code for 5 hours straight.”

✅ Good:

> “I’ll do one Pomodoro.”

![Pomodoro Timer GIF](https://media.giphy.com/media/26ufdipQqU2lhNA4g/giphy.gif)

---

### 🧩 Deep Work vs Shallow Work

**Deep Work**

* Writing complex logic
* Debugging

**Shallow Work**

* Emails

* Formatting code

🎯 Rule:

> Protect your Deep Work time like a meeting with your future self.

## 🧠 Part 2 · Learning That Actually Sticks

### 🔁 Spaced Repetition

Repetition ≠ Memorization
**Timing** = Memorization

📉 Forgetting Curve:

* You forget ~70% in 24 hours

📈 Spaced Repetition:

* Review just before forgetting
* Each review strengthens memory

![Learning Curve GIF](https://media.giphy.com/media/3o7btPCcdNniyf0ArS/giphy.gif)

---

### 📚 What Developers Should Use It For

✅ Syntax & APIs
✅ Algorithms & patterns
✅ Interview prep
✅ Architecture concepts

❌ Not ideal for:

* Creative problem solving

---

## 🔥 Part 3 · Burnout: The Silent Career Killer

Burnout is not weakness.
It’s **mismanaged energy**.

### ⚠️ Common Signs

* 😴 Constant fatigue
* 😡 Irritability
* 🧠 Brain fog
* 🚫 Loss of motivation

![Burnout GIF](https://media.giphy.com/media/3orieTz6o6bq3F0ZPy/giphy.gif)

---

### 🛑 Burnout ≠ Laziness

Lazy:

* Avoids effort

Burned out:

* Wants to work but can’t

🎯 Burnout happens when:

* High effort
* Low recovery
* Long duration

---

### 🧯 Prevention

✅ Work in sprints
✅ Real breaks (walk, stretch)
✅ Sleep is non-negotiable
✅ Exercise regularly
✅ Set boundaries

![Rest GIF](https://media.giphy.com/media/26gsqQxPQXHBiBEUU/giphy.gif)
