## 🧠 Part 1 · Resumes That Get Interviews

### 📄 The Purpose of a Resume

A resume is **not** your life story.

It’s a marketing document with one goal:

🎯 Get the interview.

❌ Bad resume:

* Long paragraphs
* Generic responsibilities
* Buzzwords only

✅ Good resume:

* Clear impact
* Measurable results
* Relevant projects

---

### 🧱 Resume Structure

```text
Name + Title
Short summary (2–3 lines)
Key skills
Experience / Projects
Education (if relevant)
```

💡 If you don’t have experience yet, **projects ARE experience**.

---

### ✍️ Write Impact, Not Tasks

❌ Bad:

> “Worked on backend services.”

✅ Good:

> “Designed and implemented REST APIs used by 10k+ users, reducing response time by 30%.”

Numbers tell stories.

## 🤝 Part 2 · Networking Without Being Fake

### 🧠 What Networking Really Is

Networking is not:

* Asking for favors
* Spamming LinkedIn
* Forced small talk

Networking **is**:

* Building genuine relationships
* Sharing interests
* Being useful

🎯 Rule:

> People help people they know and trust.

---

### 🌍 Where Networking Happens

* Meetups & conferences
* Open source communities
* Twitter / LinkedIn
* Discord / Slack groups
* Former colleagues

💡 Opportunities often come from **weak ties**, not close friends.

![Networking GIF](https://media.giphy.com/media/3o7btPCcdNniyf0ArS/giphy.gif)

---

### 💬 Simple Networking Message

```text
Hi Ana,
I saw your talk about mobile architecture and really liked
how you explained trade-offs in real projects.
I’m currently working on similar problems and would love to stay in touch.
```

❌ Don’t ask for a job.
✅ Start a conversation.

## 💼 Part 3 · Freelancing as a Career Accelerator

Freelancing is not just about money.

It gives you:

* Real clients
* Real deadlines
* Real constraints
* Real confidence

---

### ⚖️ Freelancing: Pros & Cons

✅ Pros:

* Fast learning
* Portfolio growth
* Flexibility

❌ Cons:

* Income variability
* Client management
* Responsibility

💡 Freelancing teaches skills jobs don’t.

![Freelance GIF](https://media.giphy.com/media/26ufdipQqU2lhNA4g/giphy.gif)

---

### 🧾 First Freelance Projects

Good first gigs:

* Fixing bugs
* Small features
* MVPs
* Automation scripts

Avoid at first:

* Huge undefined projects
* “Equity only” offers

🎯 Start small. Deliver well.
