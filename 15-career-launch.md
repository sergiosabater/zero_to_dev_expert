## 🧠 Part 1 · Resumes That Get Interviews

<br>

### 📄 The Purpose of a Resume

A resume is **not** your life story.

It's a **marketing document** with one goal:

<div align="center">

### 🎯 Get the interview.

</div>

<br>

| ❌ Bad resume | ✅ Good resume |
|---------------|----------------|
| Long paragraphs | Clear impact |
| Generic responsibilities | Measurable results |
| Buzzwords only | Relevant projects |

---

<br>

### 🧱 Resume Structure

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│  Name + Title                   │
├─────────────────────────────────┤
│  Short summary (2–3 lines)      │
├─────────────────────────────────┤
│  Key skills                     │
├─────────────────────────────────┤
│  Experience / Projects          │
├─────────────────────────────────┤
│  Education (if relevant)        │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

<br>

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** If you don't have experience yet, **projects ARE experience**.

---

<br>

### ✍️ Write Impact, Not Tasks

<br>

#### ❌ Bad:
```
"Worked on backend services."
```

#### ✅ Good:
```
"Designed and implemented REST APIs used by 10k+ users,
reducing response time by 30%."
```

<br>

<div align="center">

**Numbers tell stories.**

## 🤝 Part 2 · Networking Without Being Fake

<br>

### 🧠 What Networking Really Is

<br>

#### Networking is NOT:
- ❌ Asking for favors
- ❌ Spamming LinkedIn
- ❌ Forced small talk

#### Networking IS:
- ✅ Building genuine relationships
- ✅ Sharing interests
- ✅ Being useful

<br>

<div align="center">

### 🎯 Golden Rule:
> *People help people they know and trust.*

</div>

---

<br>

### 🌍 Where Networking Happens

<br>

- 🎤 **Meetups & conferences**
- 🌟 **Open source communities**
- 💼 **Twitter / LinkedIn**
- 💬 **Discord / Slack groups**
- 🤝 **Former colleagues**

<br>

> 💡 **Insight:** Opportunities often come from **weak ties**, not close friends.

<br>

<div align="center">

![Networking GIF](https://media.giphy.com/media/3o7btPCcdNniyf0ArS/giphy.gif)

</div>

---

<br>

### 💬 Simple Networking Message

<br>

```text
Hi Ana,

I saw your talk about mobile architecture and really liked
how you explained trade-offs in real projects.

I'm currently working on similar problems and would love 
to stay in touch.
```

<br>

<div align="center">

| ❌ Don't | ✅ Do |
|----------|-------|
| Ask for a job | Start a conversation |

</div>
